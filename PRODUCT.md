# iCAM Video Telematics — Product Truth

## What it is
iCAM Video Telematics is a proudly South African specialist that supplies, installs, maintains and monitors vehicle video + telematics systems for commercial fleets. Founded 2015 (reg 2015/280726/07). Taglines: **"Get The Full Picture"** and **"Your Future Starts With Us"**.

## The mechanism (one sentence)
It fuses in-vehicle HD video with GPS, sensor and AI-safety data into one platform, so fleet operators get visual evidence *and* context for every event — not just more data points.

## Audience & scene
Fleet, transport, construction and mining operators running vehicles 24/7, often cross-border. Decision-makers: fleet/operations managers, risk & safety managers, owners. They care about driver safety, road safety, incident evidence, recovery, and fleet optimisation.

## Footprint (important — do not misstate)
- **Two head offices only: Pretoria and Cape Town.** No physical offices elsewhere.
- They **support fleets operating cross-border**, all the way up to the DRC (Lesotho, Eswatini, Botswana, Namibia, Zimbabwe, Mozambique, Zambia, Malawi, Angola, DRC, Tanzania, etc.). Coverage ≠ offices.

## Product stack (confirmed)
- **Vehicle Video Systems (MDVR):** 1–16 camera channels; VGA → Full HD 1080P; 64GB–2TB onboard (~900 hrs); embedded GPS, Wi-Fi, 4G (SIM), accelerometer; live stream, event/alarm clips, historical footage.
- **Tracking & Telematics:** vehicle/machine/asset/trailer; CANBUS + 3rd-party (fuel probes, temperature); driver ID/RFID, two-way comms, satellite; speed, idle, route history, trips.
- **Driver Fatigue & ADAS:** fatigue, distraction, mobile-phone-use, headway, collision, pedestrian warnings; facial recognition on the fatigue camera.
- **Platform:** web, desktop, iOS & Android; integrated telematics + video + fleet management.
- **Reports & BI:** 450+ report layouts, up to 10,000 data fields; customised driver behaviour & scoring.
- **24/7 Bureau:** vehicle recovery, alarm monitoring, incident management, video monitoring packages; 1st & 2nd-tier technical support, 24/7.
