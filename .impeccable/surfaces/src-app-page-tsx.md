## Direction contract

THESIS: A scroll-told brand story for a fleet-safety company, carried by large verified imagery and quiet editorial typography — the page *shows* fleets at work and reveals meaning as you scroll, instead of stacking feature cards. It refuses the SaaS-landing template (hero + 3 feature cards + logo wall + CTA band) and refuses eyebrow kickers, boxed cards-next-to-text, and dashboard chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white paper ground (#F6F3EE), warm near-black ink (#17140F), a single deep-ink-blue accent (iCAM #0A1A7A family) used sparingly — never terracotta/red, never gradient text. Display set in a high-contrast editorial serif (Libre Caslon Display); everything else in a clean grotesque (Hanken Grotesk). Thin 1px hairlines, enormous whitespace, full-bleed photography with clip-path reveals and gentle parallax. No glass, no glow, no cards as structure.

STORY: Visitor lands on a cinematic-but-calm statement of the stakes ("Get The Full Picture"), scrolls through the company's reason-to-exist, then the capability story told as verbs (See → Know → Prevent → Understand → Respond), then who it's for (vehicle types), then proof (CE/ISO, 24/7, 450+), and ends on a human close + contact. They understand iCAM fuses video+telematics+AI safety, believe it's serious and proven, and reach the contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed road/fleet photograph, warm-graded, held behind a thin top bar (wordmark left, one text link + "Get in touch" right). Lower-left: an oversized serif headline "Get the full picture." in 2–3 lines with a clip-path line-reveal; one quiet line of grotesque subcopy beneath; a single understated text link "Scroll" with a hairline. Image drifts slowly (parallax) and the grain/vignette is warm. No buttons shouting, no card.

FORM: Image-led editorial scrollytelling (sticky-image + scrubbed text scenes). User-PINNED direction (editorial light & premium, "very Emil Kowalski"); no concept-seed roll — pinned beats the roll. Code-led build (no comp image generation available). Lenis smooth scroll already in project.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
